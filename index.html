About

Webmaster Designer with a background in full-stack web development, skilled in managing, maintaining, and improving websites. Experienced in content management, web integration, website optimization, and enhancing user experience.

My work sits between content, design, development, and problem-solving. I manage website updates, structure content, integrate tools and services, refine responsive layouts, troubleshoot issues, and improve the details users often never notice — because they simply work.

Before joining Linkeo, I completed Le Wagon’s intensive full-stack web development bootcamp, where I built and deployed two MVPs in agile teams. This experience gave me a strong development foundation and helped me understand the importance of combining technical skills with practical problem-solving.

I believe a website isn’t finished when it launches. It needs to be maintained, tested, adapted, and continuously improved. My approach is simple: keep things clear, keep things reliable, and always leave the website better than I found it.
